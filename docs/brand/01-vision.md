# 01 — Vision

*The founding document. Everything else answers to it.*
*Version 2 · 3 August 2026*

---

## The truth

Most of life repeats. Mornings, weeks, work. The world has become very good at serving what repeats — faster, cheaper, more available, more the same.

We stop noticing what repeats.

What people carry is the small number of things that happened once. A day. A room. A first night. An hour they will still be describing forty years later.

Those things get handed the same tools as everything else. The tools are not the problem. The default is — whatever gets chosen when nobody decides. A default is the right answer to a question that repeats, and the wrong one every other time.

Nobody remembers a moment for the information in it.

> ## What happens once deserves more than information.

## Why we had to exist

A moment does not become a memory on its own. Something has to hold it — a photograph, an object, the way a room is arranged. Someone has to decide, a year in advance, what will matter afterwards.

Most of us have been to something that was beautiful in the room and forgettable everywhere else. Months of attention go into the room and its light. Then the place where people actually arrive first — where guests are welcomed, where work is announced, where an invitation lands at midnight — is the one part nobody designed.

It carries the facts and none of the feeling.

Nobody complains about this. There is no invoice for the thing that was never made, and nobody misses what they never had. That is how it stays nobody's job.

**The world is built for what happens often. We work on what happens once.**

## What we believe

**Memory is made, not captured.** Attention is what turns a moment into one, and it has to be given in advance, deliberately, by someone.

**Feeling is structure.** It has sequence, weight and silence. It is built, or it is missing. You cannot add it at the end.

**People keep details, not summaries.** One detail, exactly right, does more than everything around it.

**Restraint is the technique.** Anyone can add. The work is in what is taken away, and in what is allowed to stay still.

**The person who arrives comes before the person who pays.** They are rarely the same person. When the two want different things, we argue for the one who will be standing there — and we say so at the start, not when it becomes awkward. It costs us work.

## What we make

For now, this work happens on screens. The internet has become functional; we create the exceptions.

The medium is not the studio. Whatever people arrive through next, the work is the same.

## What we refuse

- Work whose purpose is to transmit information. It is better served by people who love that problem.
- Volume. More than we can hold in our heads is a different business, run by different people.
- Anything judged by how many people it converted.
- Effects that exist to prove we can make effects.
- Making the same thing twice.

## How we will know

- Someone describes our work by describing how it felt, and still has the details right years later.
- Someone remembers the studio from something they came to for a completely different reason.
- Someone goes back to something we made years afterwards, for no reason except that they wanted to.
- Work we made a decade ago still holds, because none of it was fashionable.

---

This has never been anyone's job.

We made it ours.
