# 03 — Experience Principles

*How the studio thinks. Not how the current work looks.*
*Version 2 · 3 August 2026 · governed by `01-vision.md`*

---

Six principles. Each is a belief about how people experience things, so each survives a change of medium, technology or fashion.

Under every one is what it prevents. A principle that stops no bad decision is a preference.

The examples will date. The principles above them should not.

---

## 1. The arrival is designed before the contents

Someone knows where they are before they know what is there. That impression forms once, it forms fast, and everything afterwards is read through it.

So the entrance is not the last thing we style. It is the first thing we decide, and the contents are then arranged to live inside it.

**It prevents:** building the thing and then decorating its front door. Beginning with a statement, an image and an invitation to act, because that is what a beginning usually looks like.

## 2. We decide the order. They decide the pace.

A person attends to one thing at a time. When several things ask at once, the choice of what to notice falls to whoever arrived — and they make it badly, not from carelessness but because nobody can attend to two things and still feel either.

Deciding the order is our job, and it is most of the job. Deciding how long to stay is theirs, always. Anyone who wants to move faster than we imagined has to be able to.

**It prevents:** presenting everything at once and calling it choice. Sequences that cannot be skipped, durations that cannot be shortened, and any moment that holds someone in place to prove it was worth making.

## 3. Silence is material

Emptiness and stillness are not what remains when the content stops. They are the strongest instruments available, they cost nothing to produce, and they take more conviction to defend than anything else in the work.

A pause is allowed to be the entire design of a moment.

**It prevents:** filling space because it looks unfinished. Adding one more thing to something that was already working.

## 4. Everything present is doing work

An element earns its place by carrying meaning, creating a relation, or making the next thing possible. Convention is not work. Habit is not work. Looking impressive is not work.

**It prevents:** decoration mistaken for craft, movement added because something felt static, and whole sections inherited from the last hundred things that resembled this one.

## 5. We are not the subject, and we are not the audience

The subject belongs to someone else — their work, their evening, their name. We are the room it stands in, and a room that competes with what it holds has failed at being a room.

The audience is not us either. Not the person paying, not the people in the review. It is someone standing somewhere with other things on their mind.

**It prevents:** work that would still look impressive with the client's material taken out. Judging anything in conditions it will never actually meet.

## 6. Friction breaks the spell

The moment someone notices the mechanism, they are no longer inside the experience. A stutter, a wait, a control that does not answer — each costs more than any amount of beauty returns.

This is a constraint applied at the beginning, not a repair applied at the end. An idea that cannot be delivered reliably is not a good idea awaiting optimisation. It is the wrong idea.

**It prevents:** designing for ideal conditions, then defending the result with "it works on mine".

---

## Conditions

Not principles — conditions. No version of the work trades them away.

**Everyone gets a designed version.** Someone who needs less movement, no sound, or another way in receives a composed alternative that carries the same meaning. Never the same thing with parts removed.

**Sound never starts on its own.** It waits to be asked for, it can always be stopped, and everything it does the silent version must do as well.

**Nothing essential depends on the most fragile part.** If the newest, heaviest or least reliable piece never arrives, the story is still there and still in order.

**Legibility is never traded for atmosphere.** Confirmed in the conditions the work will meet, not the ones it was made in.

---

## The three questions

Asked of anything before it is accepted.

1. **What is the one thing this is for?** If the answer needs a comma, it is doing two jobs.
2. **What is lost if it goes?** If nothing, it goes.
3. **Could this belong to another project?** If yes, it is not finished.
